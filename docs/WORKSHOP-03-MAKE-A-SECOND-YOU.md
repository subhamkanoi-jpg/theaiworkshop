**The screenshot:** their kit on the phone, their name on it.

**The unlocked door:** this kit is what shops already pay ₹3,000–₹8,000 for. We do not promise they get paid on Monday.

## 3. The recipe

### Raw material (11:20)

They talk for three minutes, or write a messy paragraph:

- What they actually do on a weekday (not the LinkedIn line)
- Who they help
- Three pieces of their own writing they already like (or we lend samples)
- What the assistant must never do
- Three jobs they will give it this week

### The kit (11:45)

Everyone mirrors the projector:

1. One-line offer
2. Bio in their voice
3. Seven posts for one channel they pick (WhatsApp / Instagram / LinkedIn)
4. Paste the winning brief into Gemini → Gems. Name it. Save it.

### Three live tests (after tea)

1. **A reply** — paste a real message. The Gem answers. They edit two lines by hand.
2. **A make** — they pick their favourite post and make it theirs.
3. **A plan** — “Five jobs before Friday.” Screenshot. That is Tuesday.

If it sounds like a brochure, send them back to examples and “never do.” Do not add adjectives.
