How a Sunday works

You bring raw material — a voice note, a messy paragraph, WhatsApp chats — or use ours. The host shows the trick once on the projector. You do it at a table of eight, with a captain on the floor. You leave with the file on your phone and a recipe you can open on Tuesday. This is not a lecture. You build.

Talk, then make

  1. Talk for three minutes about your actual work.
  2. Follow the projector. Captains unblock the table.
  3. Save the artifact and the brief so you can run it again.

The Path

Five chapters. Enter on any Sunday. Leave with a thing every time. Talk: brief a model so it does useful work. Make: a named artifact (website in June, reel in August). Sell: turn the artifact into an offer someone can pay for. System: a weekday habit. Lead: captain a table, then teach. June was websites. August was reels. September is the brief underneath both — The Magic of AI.

What you walk out with on 27 September

A week of finished work made from how you actually talk: a bio, an offer line, seven posts, and the saved recipe. Default tool: Google Gemini on a free account. ChatGPT and Claude are welcome. No Premiere, no VS Code, no paid software required for this Sunday. Hosts: Yogesh Kanoi, Neeraj Kanoi, Subham Kanoi.
