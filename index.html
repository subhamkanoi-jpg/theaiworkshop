What you build

  • The business brief: one page that teaches every automation your offer, your prices, your tone, and the things it must never say
  • Lead capture: a live form that drops every enquiry into one Google Sheet, with nothing copied by hand
  • The AI first reply: a personal email in your voice, written from your brief and sent within minutes of the enquiry
  • The follow-up sequence: three emails on day 2, day 5 and day 9 that stop once the lead replies, plus the same pipeline to send a campaign to people who asked to hear from you
  • The FAQ bot: a Telegram bot that answers customer questions from your own price list, and hands the conversation to you when it is not sure

What to bring

A laptop and charger. This is four hours of building, not a talk. Bring your phone with Telegram installed, your price list or menu, the five questions customers ask you most, and the email you send when someone enquires. No business of your own yet? We hand you a sample one at the table.
