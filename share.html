Build a Complete AI Video Ad From Scratch

Walk in with an idea. Walk out at 2:00 PM with a finished vertical ad: one face-locked character, 4 to 6 production stills, motion clips, and a natural voiceover, built on one repeatable prompt system. Three hours, offline, no filmmaking background required.

What you build

  • The locked actor: one continuity-safe face reference with real skin texture, so the same person survives every scene
  • Four to six production base stills: vertical 9:16 scenes planned through explicit camera, lens, and lighting instructions
  • The motion clips: your approved stills turned into controlled video in Google Flow and Veo, with handheld movement instead of random morphing
  • A human voiceover: natural cadence built with ElevenLabs bracketed delivery cues, or your own recorded take run through voice changing
  • The recipe: the master prompt stack saved on your laptop, so the next ad starts at step one instead of a blank chat window

What to bring

A laptop and charger. This is three hours of building, not a talk. Bring headphones, your phone, and one product, service, or offer you actually sell. If you would rather practise on something else, we hand you a brief at the table.
