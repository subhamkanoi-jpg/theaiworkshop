Build Your AI Follow-up Assistant

Turn messy notes from a meeting, customer enquiry, or supplier call into a clear recap, next steps, and a message you can review and send. Build and save your own AI follow-up assistant in one hands-on, offline session. No coding.

What you build

  • Your own follow-up assistant, saved as a Gemini Gem or reusable instructions
  • One reviewed recap, action list, and email or WhatsApp draft from your notes

  • A fact-check and privacy checklist to use before sending anything
