### Do I need to know coding?

No. Every step happens in visual tools: you connect boxes, paste in your facts, and write instructions in plain English, Hindi or Bangla. If you can fill in a Google Form and send an email, you can build this. A captain sits at every table to unblock you when a connection fails.

### What is running by 4:00 PM?

One automation for your own business: a form that captures each enquiry into a Google Sheet, an AI-written first reply in your voice sent within minutes, three follow-up emails on day 2, day 5 and day 9 that stop once the lead replies, and a Telegram bot that answers from your price list. You also keep the brief, the prompts and the exported blueprint, so the second one does not start from scratch.

### Do the tools cost money?

Not on the day. The build runs on free tiers: Make’s free plan, a free Gemini API key from Google AI Studio, Gmail, Google Sheets and Telegram. Free tiers limit how often an automation runs and how much it sends, and those limits change. We show you how to stay inside them, and what the first paid upgrade would be if your volume grows. The ticket does not include any paid plan.

### Can I use my own business?

Please do. Bring your price list or menu, the five questions customers ask most, and the email you send when someone enquires. Shop owners, clinics, coaches, freelancers and agency owners get the most out of the day, because the automation is theirs on Monday. No business yet? We hand you a sample one at the table.

### Why Telegram and not WhatsApp?

Because anyone can create a working Telegram bot in two minutes, free. WhatsApp’s official bot access needs Meta business verification and charges per conversation; setting it up takes days, not an afternoon. The bot’s brain — your facts, your tone, your hand-over rule — is the same on both. We build it on Telegram and show you the path to WhatsApp once your business is verified.

### Will this spam people?

Not the way we build it. Every email goes to someone who contacted you or asked to hear from you, every sequence stops when they reply, and campaigns carry a way to opt out. We do not buy lists, scrape contacts or send cold blasts, and Gmail would block you if you tried. If you want to email ten thousand strangers, skip this Sunday.

### Can I attend with only a phone?

Not usefully. Building the automations needs a laptop browser and a real screen. Your phone is for testing: you send the fake lead and talk to your bot from it. Bring a laptop and a charger — power strips and table Wi-Fi are provided at the venue.

### What about customer data and privacy?

Build with test leads, not your real customer list. Do not bring confidential client files, and do not paste anything you would not want stored by a third-party service: free AI tiers may use what you send to improve their products. When you switch on with real customers, tell them how you will contact them, and follow your employer’s AI policy.
